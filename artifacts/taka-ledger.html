Statutory deadlines

    Client return due dates

    Derived from each client's type and year end
      How due dates are derived

      Individuals: Tax Day, 30 November following the income year. Companies: Tax Day is the 15th day of the seventh month after the income year end, or 15 September following the year end if the seventh-month date falls earlier (30 June year end → 15 January; 31 December year end → 15 September). VAT returns: 15th of the next month. Withholding returns: 31 January and 31 July. Advance tax: 15 September, 15 December, 15 March, 15 June.
